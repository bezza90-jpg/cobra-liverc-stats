COBRA SITE UPDATE — gallery viewer, driver avatar and refresh

Extract this ZIP. At the TOP LEVEL of the GitHub cobra-liverc-stats repository choose Add file > Upload files. Drag the public, scripts and .github folders from INSIDE the extracted folder onto the upload page and commit once. Do not drag the ZIP or its enclosing folder. Wait for Actions > Update and publish COBRA statistics to complete successfully, then refresh the site.

Only nine site/workflow files are included. This ZIP does not contain or overwrite public/data/car-avatars.json, your avatar images, driver-manufacturers.json, podium photo overrides or approved uploads. The workflow now passes the current adjustable-playback validation, fetches LiveRC results, generates podium artwork and deploys it. Avatar files must be committed to GitHub along with their car-avatars.json mappings in your manager upload. Manufacturer logos appear for drivers assigned a brand in driver-manufacturers.json when its matching logo is available; other slots do not invent brands. An existing real podium photograph always takes precedence over generated artwork.
