Photo guidance

Make your car easy to recognise

Photograph the whole car from the side or three-quarter view. Leave some space around the wheels and wing. Cars should travel left to right, facing mostly right, like Matt Hodges’s car below.

A photo facing left is fine. Tick Flip horizontally below and COBRA will turn it around when preparing the avatar. Background removal happens after approval; the original remains private.

Type part of a first name or surname, then choose your name from the matches. JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 6 MB. Clear side views work best.
