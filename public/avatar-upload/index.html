Photo guidance

Make your car easy to recognise

Photograph the whole car from the side or three-quarter view. Leave some space around the wheels and wing. Cars should travel left to right, facing mostly right, like Matt Hodges’s car below.

A photo facing left is fine. Tick Flip horizontally below and COBRA will turn it around when preparing the avatar. COBRA removes the background, reviews the prepared image and approves it before publication. Your original remains private.

Type part of a first name or surname, then choose your name from the matches. Junior drivers: choose the car class you race, such as 2WD or 4WD. JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 6 MB. Clear side views work best.
