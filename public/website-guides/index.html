Driver Car Avatar

Upload a clear car photo, choose your driver and class, then see approved avatars around the COBRA website.

Upload your car photo ↗Browse car avatars ↗
Read the quick guide

  2. Choose your driver and class, then select a clear photo showing the whole car, wheels and wing.
  3. Submit for approval. After processing and publishing, your avatar can appear in the gallery, tracker, podiums and replay.

07

Every lap tells a story

Lap Times & Race Positions

See each driver’s recorded lap times and follow positions lap by lap, using COBRA’s race charts.

Open race statistics ↗Open Virtual Race Replay ↗
Read the quick guide
  1. In Race statistics, choose an event and individual race. The same charts appear below Virtual Race Replay.
  2. Scroll through the drivers: names are on the left and lap times are on the right. Hover on desktop to enlarge, or open a chart in a new tab.
  3. Use View exact lap times for the numbers. Lap 1 includes the starting crossing. The position chart shows recorded position by lap, rather than synchronized race time.
  4. Use Share to Facebook for the chart’s link preview, or open the chart and download its image.

08

Find the difference

Compare Drivers

Select racers from the same race and compare their lap times together, then open or share the comparison.

Choose a race to compare ↗Compare in Virtual Replay ↗
Read the quick guide
  1. Choose a race in Statistics or Virtual Race Replay, then find Compare drivers below the race summary chart.
  2. Tick two or more names. The graph overlays their recorded lap times on one scale; colours, line styles and the legend identify each driver.
  3. Open selected comparison keeps your selection in a new tab. Open comparison in Virtual Replay carries those drivers into the animation.
  4. Share selected comparison to Facebook preserves the chosen drivers in the link. Its Facebook preview image shows the whole-race overview.

09

Watch the comparison

Replay Selected Drivers

Turn a driver comparison into a replay showing just those cars, with playback controls and the original race timing.

Try Virtual Race Replay ↗Choose a race in Statistics ↗
Read the quick guide
  1. Select drivers in Compare drivers, then press Open comparison in Virtual Replay.
  2. The same race opens with only your selected cars and their standings rows visible. Press Play to watch them together.
  3. Pause, change speed or move backwards and forwards through the race. Movement between timing crossings is estimated.
  4. Choose Fullscreen track or Fullscreen timings. On mobile, swipe left for timings and right for track, or use the panel buttons. Playback controls stay available; Close returns to the page.
  5. Positions and gaps stay relative to the full race. Press Show all drivers to restore the full field.
