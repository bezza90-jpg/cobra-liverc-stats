Make it yours

Driver Car Avatar

Upload a clear car photo, choose your driver and class, then see approved avatars around the COBRA website.

Upload your car photo ↗Browse car avatars ↗
Read the quick guide
  1. Open COBRA Data → Upload car avatar.
  2. Choose your driver and class, then select a clear photo showing the whole car, wheels and wing.
  3. Submit for approval. After processing and publishing, your avatar can appear in the gallery, tracker, podiums and replay.

Arrive prepared

Race Day

Find your Club or SWORD schedule, check the current event and read the drivers briefing before racing.

Club race schedule ↗SWORD race schedule ↗Drivers briefing ↗
Read the quick guide
  1. Open Race Day in the website menu.
  2. Choose Club race schedule or SWORD race schedule for your meeting.
  3. Follow the briefing and current-event links. Times are a guide; listen to race control on the day.

Find your race

Results

Explore race results ↗Club Series ↗SWORD Championship ↗
Read the quick guide

Know the grid

Meet the COBRA Drivers

Find a driver by name, recognise their cars and enlarge the photographs to see the details.

Meet the drivers ↗Driver distance tracker ↗
Read the quick guide
  1. Open COBRA Data → Driver car avatars.
  2. Search by first name or surname to find a driver.
  3. Tap a car image for a larger view. On a computer, hover or focus the image to preview it.

Explore the circuit

Track Design

Explore the public Track Gallery, then see event layouts in Virtual Race Replay. This guide covers the layouts and replay available on the live site.

Explore the Track Gallery ↗Try Virtual Race Replay ↗
Read the quick guide
  1. Open About COBRA → Track gallery to explore the carpet, jumps and technical sections.
  2. Open Virtual Race Replay and choose an event to see its available track plan.
  3. Choose a driver and press Play. Cars use recorded lap times; positions between crossings are estimates.
