Before you race

Driver Briefing

A clear guide for drivers and guests at COBRA. Please read the instructions for your particular event as well.

On the day: Check your event booking for arrival and start times. Race control will confirm the practice, heat and final schedule and any venue changes.

01 · At the venue

General housekeeping

  • On arrival, identify the toilets, designated smoking area and fire exits. Ask race control or venue staff if you cannot find them.
  • Find out where the defibrillator, first-aid kit and fire blanket are located.
  • Report an injury or fire to race control or venue staff immediately. For an emergency, call 999 and follow venue instructions.

02 · Keep the area safe

Pit area

  • Do not cut tyres directly onto the floor. Use a suitable mat or container and collect the waste.
  • Clean up oil spills promptly and report them to race control or venue staff.
  • Put all rubbish in the bins provided and leave your pit space tidy.

03 · The schedule

Race day format

  • Check your event booking for the day's start and arrival times.
  • Race control will announce the practice format, heat order, breaks and finals for that meeting.
  • Follow the live race order and listen for any changes announced on the day.

04 · On the rostrum

Driver rules

  • Wait behind race control until the previous heat has finished.
  • Take one practice lap, then bring your car to the start line as instructed.
  • If another car is clearly quicker or is lapping you, let it pass safely.
  • Use the rostrum's designated way up and separate way down.
  • Call “Car on Straight” clearly when needed.
  • Race with respect. Do not swear at other racers.
  • When you are called as finished, keep clear of drivers still racing.
  • After your race, put your car straight on the designated table, then go out to marshal as instructed.

05 · Helping others race

Marshalling

  • Wear a high-visibility vest; do not use a blue or black vest.
  • Marshal others as you would like to be marshalled. Stay alert and move safely.
  • Do not use a mobile phone within the track area while marshalling.
  • Avoid conversations with other marshals or spectators while on duty.
  • Do not eat or drink while marshalling.
  • A substitute marshal must be a BRCA member; confirm any substitution with race control.

Questions?

Ask race control

If you are unsure about an instruction, your heat or a safety point, ask race control before going onto the track. See our venue and travel information.
