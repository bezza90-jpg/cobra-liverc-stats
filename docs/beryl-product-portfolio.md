# Beryl Media Productions product and pricing plan

This plan treats the reusable systems as products owned and supplied by **Beryl Media Productions**. COBRA remains the reference club and its private data, branding and Grand Prix of Wales workflows are not part of the commercial products.

The prices below are starting recommendations, not promises to every customer. Quote after confirming data volume, integrations, branding, support and migration work. Wix, domains, payment fees, email, storage and any other supplier costs are paid directly by the club.

The replacement/custom value is what a club might reasonably spend commissioning and testing an equivalent bespoke system. It is not the selling price. Beryl can charge less because the reusable product already exists and improvements can be shared safely across clients.

## Standalone Check-in offer

Check-in should be available without buying RaceHub Club. The host club supplies a supported booking export or integration and owns the attendee data.

### Included

- Club branding and event configuration.
- Import or synchronisation from the agreed booking source.
- One personal arrival link per entrant.
- Required briefing acknowledgement before check-in.
- Live organiser board, search, manual check-in/undo and replacement-link handling.
- Duplicate protection, status history, retry handling and last-known-good operation.
- Mobile-first participant and organiser views.
- Administrator handover, privacy configuration and launch checks.

### Boundaries

- One standard booking-source integration is included; a new connector is quoted separately.
- The club remains responsible for the briefing content, lawful attendee instructions and race-day decisions.
- SMS, premium email, hardware, onsite staffing and payment processing are not included.
- Race-weekend cover is pre-booked and separately charged.

### Cost and workload model

| Work | Expected Beryl effort | How the price covers it |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| First club setup | 10–18 hours | Setup fee |
| Repeat setup using an existing connector | 5–9 hours | Setup fee and reusable licence value |
| Routine annual maintenance per club | 4–8 hours | Monthly/annual service fee |
| Shared product maintenance | 20–40 hours per year across all clients | Pooled recurring revenue |
| New booking connector or bespoke workflow | 6–20+ hours | Quoted enhancement at the current hourly rate |
| Pre-booked remote event cover | Fixed agreed window | **£150 half-day / £275 full day** |

At **£35/month**, a standard client contributes £420/year toward monitoring, compatibility work and support. Do not include unlimited event-day assistance in that figure.

## Track Designer commercial model

The Track Designer is a Beryl Media Productions product, not a COBRA-owned product. Before selling it, replace COBRA branding with configurable Beryl/product branding and separate venue-specific inventories into licensed club profiles.

### Recommended licences

- **Club perpetual licence — £695:** one club, local and hosted use, 12 months of updates and email support.
- **Updates/support renewal — £149/year:** continued feature/compatibility updates and up to one hour of support.
- **Subscription — £39/month:** current hosted/local package while subscribed, updates and basic support.
- **Founding club — £495 first year, then £99/year:** limited offer for the first reference customer in return for structured feedback and permission to use non-confidential screenshots/testimonial.
- **Custom venue/inventory setup — £250–£750:** measured carpet, custom ramps/furniture, stock rules, logo and presentation theme.
- **Bespoke layout design service — £250–£600 per layout:** optional human design service, separate from the software licence.

Free and very cheap consumer tools prevent the editor being priced like CAD software per user. Its commercial advantage is the specialist RC workflow: true venue scale, safe ribbon editing, physical furniture and stock, printable/presentation output, editable reuse and 3D review. Sell that saved club labour and consistency, not just access to a drawing canvas.

### Maintenance allowance

- Budget **20–40 hours per year** for browser compatibility, shared fixes, packaging, documentation and regression checks.
- Allow **2–4 hours per new club profile** for branding, inventory and acceptance testing after measurements are supplied.
- Charge measurement cleanup, new furniture modelling, custom exports and onsite layout work separately.
- Keep a supported-version policy: current release plus the previous major release, rather than repairing every old snapshot indefinitely.

## RaceHub Club positioning

RaceHub Club competes neither with a basic brochure-site template nor directly with race-control software. It combines:

- club-branded web presence and mobile navigation;
- Wix Events booking/payment workflows;
- event capacity and public entry information;
- LiveRC discovery, processing, archives and driver statistics;
- simplified club administration;
- monitoring, recovery and privacy controls.

RCGTiming advertises club timing at $19.99/month and national timing at $39.99/month. RC-Timing advertises £415 for its 2025 full licence plus £75 for a two-year results-broadcast subscription. These are useful lower-bound comparisons for specialist RC software, but they do not include a bespoke club website, Wix integration, migration or Beryl's management layer.

The recommended **£2,250 setup + £119/month** remains below a one-off custom build while providing enough margin for onboarding, monitoring and accountable support. For a team member, **£1,500 + £79/month** is a meaningful discount without turning the work into an unpaid obligation.

Publish business-hours response targets, not 24/7 availability. Included support time does not roll over. A fault is behaviour that no longer matches the agreed specification; a new workflow, redesign, connector or reporting request is chargeable work.
