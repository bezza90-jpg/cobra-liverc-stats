# Suggested managed RaceHub pricing

These are recommended starting prices for a UK club. Wix, the domain, payment processing and any paid third-party services should be contracted and paid directly by the club. The figures below are fees charged by **Beryl Media Productions** for RaceHub setup, licensing, management and support.

## Initial setup

**RaceHub Basic launch: £995–£1,495**

Includes club branding, domain/deployment, Wix Events connection, standard booking fields, entry/class-capacity synchronisation, LiveRC data import, current-event/basic results pages, automated checks and administrator handover. It excludes avatars, replays, setup uploads, podium galleries, advanced comparisons, GPW tools and bespoke scoring.

**Check-in add-on: £295 setup + £20/month**

Adds personal arrival links, briefing acknowledgement, replacement-link requests and a live organiser arrival board. Include it at no extra monthly charge in the semi-managed and managed-partner tiers if that makes the package easier to sell. GPW systems are not included or licensed as part of any package.

**Car Avatar Upload add-on: £395 setup + £30/month**

Adds the branded mobile/desktop upload page, driver and class selection, photograph guidance, private submission storage, administrator review, approval/rejection notifications and publication into supported RaceHub views. The monthly price includes up to 10 new or replacement images per month. Additional images are **£5 each**, or quoted as a batch for a large launch. Bulk historical imports and difficult manual image correction are separately chargeable.

For clubs that want to perform all review and image preparation themselves, offer a **self-managed avatar option at £295 setup + £15/month**. The club remains responsible for checking orientation, full-car framing, background quality, rights/consent and final approval.

**Standard launch: £1,995**

Includes a standalone repository and deployment, branding, responsive setup, domain connection assistance, Wix Events connection, standard booking fields, payment/test-order checks, Google service separation, LiveRC connection, basic archive import, administrator handover and launch checks.

Quote **£2,750–£4,000** where the club needs a large historical import, bespoke championships/scoring, complex ticket rules, custom email journeys, extensive content migration or new integrations.

Do not absorb Wix subscriptions, domain purchases, payment fees, premium email, advertising or paid media storage into this setup fee.

- **£60–£100 per event** as an alternative for clubs on low-managed service that want you to configure and verify a particular Wix event.
- Annual prepayment may be offered at the price of 11 months, but only after the first successful quarter.

## Commercial safeguards

- Minimum three-month support term after launch.
- Define a bug as behaviour that no longer matches the agreed specification; new requests are chargeable enhancements.
- State response targets rather than guaranteeing instant resolution of Wix, Google, LiveRC, banking or internet outages.
- The club owns its accounts, data and funds and pays suppliers directly.
- Your administrator access may be removed when the agreement ends; provide an export/handover process.
- Review prices annually and quote VAT separately if/when applicable.
- Publish business-hours response targets appropriate to a side business; do not imply 24/7 availability.
- Price pre-booked race-weekend cover separately and define its exact availability window.
- Cap the initial number of supported clubs until self-service, monitoring and recovery automation have proven reliable.

This is not an ordinary brochure website. The fee reflects the booking/payment path, race-data automation, monitoring, mobile testing and the availability of a maintainer who understands the complete system.
