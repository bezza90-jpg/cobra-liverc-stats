# GPW offer privacy wording

Include this section in every Grand Prix of Wales offer email before the payment link.

## Public RaceHub information

If you accept this offer and complete payment, RaceHub may publicly display the driver's name, race class, chassis and, where an approved image is available, their car avatar on GPW entry lists and race results.

For Junior Racers, only the driver's first name and surname initial will be displayed. Contact GrandPrixofWales@gmail.com to request a correction, raise a privacy concern or ask for an avatar to be removed.

The offer email must also retain the usual sender identity and marketing opt-out wording.
