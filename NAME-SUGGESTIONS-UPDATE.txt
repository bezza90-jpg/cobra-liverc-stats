COBRA car avatar: type-to-search driver names
============================================

1. Extract this ZIP. At the TOP LEVEL of your GitHub cobra-liverc-stats repository, choose Add file > Upload files and drag the extracted public FOLDER. Confirm the paths are public/avatar-upload/index.html, public/assets/avatar-upload.js and public/assets/styles.css. Commit and wait for the action to succeed.
2. Refresh the car avatar upload page. Type part of your name (including a surname); a matching-name box appears immediately below the field. Click or tap your name. Arrow keys and Enter also work.
3. If you use the desktop Site Manager, copy the same three public files into its existing public folder so its next export retains this update. Do not replace public/data/avatar-upload-config.json or redeploy Apps Script for this fix.
